Development roadmap
===================

The `research qualification plan <https://github.com/uwplasma/DKX/blob/main/plan.md>`_
is the sole authoritative queue, with scientific acceptance criteria, retained
contracts, and deferred work. It prioritizes trustworthy admission and
uncertainty-controlled results before broader performance and optimization claims.

Use :doc:`capabilities` for supported interfaces and evidence, and
:doc:`experiments/README` for measured decisions and rejected experiments.
